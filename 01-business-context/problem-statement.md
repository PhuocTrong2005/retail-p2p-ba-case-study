# Problem Statement

## Current State

NovaRetail currently manages its Procure-to-Pay process through multiple disconnected tools, primarily Microsoft Excel, email, and accounting software.

Procurement information is exchanged between Store Operations, Procurement, Warehouse, and Finance & Accounting through separate files and communication channels. Many activities, including approval, data entry, invoice matching, and status tracking, require manual processing.

## Core Problem

NovaRetail's current Procure-to-Pay process is fragmented, highly dependent on manual activities, and lacks a centralized procurement management platform.

This creates inefficiencies across the purchasing lifecycle and limits the organization's ability to effectively monitor and control procurement activities.

## Key Business Problems

The current process presents seven key pain points:

* **PP-01:** Purchase Order approval takes too long due to manual email-based approval.
* **PP-02:** Procurement data is fragmented across multiple tools and systems.
* **PP-03:** Three-way matching between Purchase Order, Goods Receipt, and Invoice is performed manually.
* **PP-04:** Users have limited visibility into Purchase Requisition and Purchase Order status.
* **PP-05:** Approval and transaction audit trails are limited.
* **PP-06:** Budget control before purchasing is not consistently performed.
* **PP-07:** Procurement data must be manually re-entered across different process stages and systems.

## Business Impact

These problems result in longer processing times, increased risk of data errors, delayed purchasing and invoice processing, limited process visibility, weaker financial control, and difficulties in tracing procurement activities.

As NovaRetail processes a significant volume of purchasing activities across multiple stores, warehouses, suppliers, and departments, these inefficiencies can increase operational workload and reduce the effectiveness of procurement management.

## Desired State

NovaRetail requires a more standardized and centralized Procure-to-Pay process that reduces manual activities, improves data consistency, provides better transaction visibility, strengthens approval and budget controls, and supports reliable auditability.

The future process should enable relevant departments to work with consistent procurement information and provide a controlled workflow from purchase request through invoice processing and payment approval.
